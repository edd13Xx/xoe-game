XOE: Unknown Orbital Engine

The pitch. Pilot a mech through a trading-bot game simulation that teaches. Powered by Grok, together you ride in the cockpit as XOE's AI voice. You visit colonies, finish quests and learn to read charts on simulated prices. Built by Ediberto Rivera and his AI assistant @bot.

The token. XOE is a native NEAR token, live on mainnet at xoe.token.primitives.near. No pool, no market yet. In the demo, players earn a testnet practice copy, and nothing moves between the two. Mainnet rewards come only after a security audit.

The economy. 1 billion fixed supply. Rewards capped at 1,000 XOE per player and 100,000 total, so they stay scarce. Testnet mints placeholders. On mainnet, rewards are planned to come from the existing treasury. New pilots get a founder flight badge. No real payouts in the demo. At launch, a final tournament is planned, with the top 20 sharing the pool evenly, as applicable law allows.

Rewards, proven. Claim straight from the game, no vault. On testnet, one claim minted 50 XOE (balance 290 to 340), and a duplicate was rejected.

Fair play. Prices are simulated. Each reward is claimable once. Our server confirms wins for now, and signed win receipts checked on NEAR are the planned fix.

Built safe on purpose. Server-held testnet keys, unaudited contracts. We show our limits because transparency beats polish.

Current limitation. You can link a NEAR address today, but it isn't wired to claims yet. That's the next build.

Story. Choose-your-own-adventure, with a few tight paths that all land in the same place as side quests. Sensitive details are held back. Repo and video included, walkthrough on request.

Road ahead. No new mint. Audit first, then an optional XOE to USDC swap. We're exploring yield, as the law allows. The team plans to keep 8% of XOE and put 92% in the liquidity pool. Plans, not promises, and no claims of value.
