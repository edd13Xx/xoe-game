**Who built it.** XOE was built by Eddie Rivera and his AI assistant, Bot. The full storyline is already written.

**What it is.** XOE is a native NEAR token, live on NEAR mainnet at xoe.token.primitives.near. The game is a trading-bot game simulation that teaches. You pilot a mech, clear colonies, and learn to read charts on simulated prices, with the reward flow running on NEAR testnet.

**The Grok coach.** Grok rides in the cockpit as XOE's AI voice. Every tip is an urgent battle warning that opens with an XO/XOE callout. If Grok doesn't answer within six seconds, a rule-based backup coach takes over, so play never stalls.

**Teaching without jargon.** RSI and EMA are calculated from the sim prices, but the player never hears those terms or their numbers. Enemies hide in the RSI levels. Downside risk is a monster or a black hole below. A low balance means low oxygen: one more hit and you're destroyed. Players learn to ride charts by surviving, not by memorizing indicators.

**Honest caveats.** This is an early-development demo. Prices are simulated, and rewards are testnet-only placeholder amounts. The demo uses server-held testnet keys and the contracts have not been audited.

**Rewards by design.** Players claim XOE straight from the game, with no vault. A player links a NEAR address on our rewards contract (rewards-v2.xoe-game.testnet), the game server attests an achievement, the player claims it, and the contract mints testnet XOE. Verified on testnet: a claim minted 50 XOE (290 to 340) and a second claim was rejected. Today the claim mints to the claiming testnet account. Paying straight to the linked address is the next build step, and no mainnet rewards are live.

**Road ahead.** In a later phase, an XOE to USDC liquidity pool will let players swap rewards, using the same reward contracts with a new mint and pool. No real money moves through any swap until a security audit is done.
