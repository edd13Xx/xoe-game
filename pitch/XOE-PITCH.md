# XOE: Unknown Orbital Engine
### NEAR HACKATHON 2026 pitch (Games With Real Economies)

XOE is a Solana coin on Pump Fun paired with NEAR. It's a small project we've kept quiet because it runs on cutting-edge technology, with Grok AI woven into the storyline itself. Grok isn't just a tool here. It's part of the narrative, guiding the mech pilot's journey, and in the demo the trading coach inside the simulation is a live Grok API call that reacts to the player's paper trades (with a rule-based fallback so the game never breaks). The simulation teaches players how to trade using paper trading tools.

XOE already has an in-depth storyline: a mech pilot's journey across the colonies, then the moons Sol and Xelm toward the Ragnarok gate, while a spiritual-mechanical entity approaches the planet.

We found the NEAR Hackathon just as it was starting, and the deadline lands tomorrow at 5:59 PM Central. In that short window we've already put in serious work and built multiple working demos, including a live NEAR testnet economy where clearing a colony in the game is recorded on-chain and rewards the player with XOE tokens.

Our vision is to build alongside the most prominent AI-driven blockchain in the space, NEAR Protocol. We paired the token with NEAR on purpose, so the token itself represents the vision and the trading simulation.

Why would a first-time builder pick Solana, NEAR and Grok together, when any one of them alone would have been enough? Because together they bring humans and AI into one educational trading tool built on advanced technology, while players earn rewards through Wrapped NEAR.

Win or lose, XOE is building on NEAR. That was the plan before we ever heard about this hackathon, and it stays the plan after.
